## A. Categorization of Findings

This audit explicitly distinguishes between:
- **Genuine Design / Slop Problems (`Fix Required`):** Visually or editorially undermines academic credibility, hierarchy, accessibility, or mobile ergonomics.
- **Accessibility / Mobile Functional Gaps (`Fix Required`):** Affects touch targets (`< 44px`), sticky viewport consumption, or prose measure.
- **Intentional Existing Design Choices (`Preserved`):** Deliberate engineering & pedagogical decisions backed by Phase 22–23 architecture.
- **Purely Aesthetic Preferences (`Untouched`):** Subjective alternatives that would cause gratuitous churn without functional gain.
